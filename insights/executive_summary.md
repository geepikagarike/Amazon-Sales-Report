## 3 Core Strategic Takeaways for Leadership

1. **Protect and Monetize High-Value VIPs**: 78.93% of revenue stems from 2,389 VIP customers. Retention and loyalty initiatives here will yield outsized returns.
2. **Mitigate Pre-Shipment Cancellation Leakage**: Recovering even one-third of the INR 6.89M lost to cancellations through faster Amazon FBA fulfillment represents an immediate INR ~2.3M top-line opportunity.
3. **Rebalance Inventory & Product Sizing**: Reallocate 70% of manufacturing capacity to Medium and Large sizes while bundling slow-moving accessories with top-selling T-shirts and Shirts.
