# Strategic Business Recommendations

Based on empirical data analysis across **128,017 orders and INR 69.39M in revenue**, here are actionable recommendations categorized by business function:

---

## 1. Customer Retention & Growth Strategies

| Strategic Initiative | Target Segment | Expected Business Impact | Implementation Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VIP Loyalty Club ("Prime Elite")** | VIP Platinum (Top 16.1% buyers) | Protects 78.9% of company revenue; reduces high-value customer churn by 15-20% | Offer free expedited 1-day delivery, VIP concierge support, and early access to festival sales. |
| **Second-Order Reactivation Campaign** | Occasional Bronze (41.6% buyers) | Increases repeat customer rate from 30.2% to 38%; adds ~INR 1.8M incremental revenue | Send triggered WhatsApp/email discount codes (15% off next order) 10 days post-delivery. |
| **B2B Bulk Purchase Portal** | B2B Wholesale Accounts | Expands B2B revenue contribution from 0.79% to 5.0% | Introduce bulk pricing tiers (5+ units at 15% wholesale discount) and tax invoicing checkout. |

## 2. Merchandising & Product Strategy

| Strategic Initiative | Target Category / SKU | Expected Business Impact | Implementation Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apparel + Accessory Cross-Selling Bundles** | T-shirts & Shirts + Wallets/Perfumes | Increases Average Order Value (AOV) from INR 577 to INR 650+ | Implement "Frequently Bought Together" bundles on Amazon product detail pages with 20% accessory discounts. |
| **Inventory Size Rebalancing** | Sizes M, L, XL vs 4XL-6XL | Reduces out-of-stock events on top sizes by 40%; frees up working capital | Allocate 70% of manufacturing procurement to sizes M and L; limit 4XL-6XL to on-demand batches. |
| **Tail SKU Rationalization** | Watch Category & Low-Traction Footwear | Eliminates catalog clutter and listing overhead | Liquidate remaining watch and footwear inventory through clearance deals. |

## 3. Supply Chain & Logistics Strategy

| Strategic Initiative | Target Channel / Geography | Expected Business Impact | Implementation Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Transition to 90%+ Amazon FBA** | Merchant Easy Ship Orders | Cuts cancellation rate from 14.1% to < 9%; recovers ~INR 2.3M in lost GMV | Move merchant-fulfilled SKU stock into Amazon regional fulfillment centers in Mumbai and Bengaluru. |
| **Regional Inventory Pre-positioning** | Top 5 States (MH, KA, TS, UP, TN) | Lowers shipping transit time to < 24-48 hours for 60% of national orders | Distribute 65% of warehouse safety stock across Western and Southern fulfillment hubs. |
