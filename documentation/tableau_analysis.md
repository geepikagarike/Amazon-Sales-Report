# Tableau Visual Analytics & Dashboard Storytelling

## 1. Role of Tableau in the Analytics Stack
While Power BI acts as the quantitative modeling and calculation backbone, **Tableau is deployed for interactive visual analytics, executive storytelling, and exploratory discovery**.

---

## 2. Four Interactive Tableau Dashboards

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                TABLEAU EXECUTIVE STORYTELLING                                 |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. Executive Sales Overview       -->  High-level commercial pulse (Revenue, AOV, Growth)   |
|  2. Customer Intelligence          -->  RFM Segmentation, VIP spenders, Repeat buyer rate     |
|  3. Product & Sizing Performance   -->  80/20 category rule, Top vs Bottom SKUs, Size curves  |
|  4. Sales Trend & Logistics        -->  Daily volatility, FBA vs Easy Ship, Cancellation maps |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Dashboard 1: Executive Sales Overview
* **Key Visuals**: KPI Banner, Dual-Axis Monthly Revenue & Order Volume, Category Donut, Top 10 Products, Geographic Heatmap.
* **Target Persona**: Chief Executive Officer (CEO) & VP of Sales.

### Dashboard 2: Customer Analytics
* **Key Visuals**: RFM Segment Treemap, Top 15 Customer Ranking, Purchase Frequency Distribution, Customer CLV vs Order Frequency Quadrants.
* **Target Persona**: Chief Marketing Officer (CMO) & Retention Marketing Leads.

### Dashboard 3: Product Performance & Inventory
* **Key Visuals**: Category vs Sizing Heatmap Matrix, Top 10 vs Tail 10 Product SKUs, Price vs Volume Bubble Plot.
* **Target Persona**: Merchandising Director & Inventory Planner.

### Dashboard 4: Sales Trends & Logistics Diagnostics
* **Key Visuals**: Continuous Area Sales Trajectory, Day-of-Week Checkout Heatmap, Fulfillment Funnel by Region, Channel Delivery Latency.
* **Target Persona**: Chief Operating Officer (COO) & Logistics Manager.
